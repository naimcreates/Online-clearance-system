Clearance Guidelines

Official procedures for the Fall 2025 Semester

📚 Library Clearance

All borrowed books must be returned 7 days prior to the final exam. Late fees are calculated at TK 50/day.

🧪 Laboratory Dues

Breakage fees must be paid at the Bursar office. Get the receipt signed by the Lab Technician.

🏠 Hall Clearance

Room handover checklist must be signed by the Provost. Keys must be returned to the caretaker.

🚨 Emergency Protocol

Emergency clearance requires Registrar approval and has a yearly quota.
